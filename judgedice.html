Section 02 · Home Life

The exhale after the resume.

When I’m not untangling enterprise tech stacks, I’m probably driving someone to band practice, arguing about something mundane, or thinking about work anyway.

I have a family that puts up with me. I’ve been in the Midwest long enough to have opinions about the winters. I believe strongly in the power of a good lunch — there is no competing with free lunch, the gravity is too strong.

I’ve been on the internet since before it had a GUI. Somehow that became a career. Still figuring out if that was a good thing.

The truth is I got into this work because I liked building things that didn’t exist yet. That’s still true. The tools have changed a lot — Flash is dead, long live the API — but the feeling of shipping something that works is the same.
